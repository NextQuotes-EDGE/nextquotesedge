## Overview

The CONMSIT School Management System is a full-stack platform built for the College of Nursing Sciences, Calabar. It combines a public-facing website, a standalone student and staff portal, and a hardened REST backend into one npm-workspace monorepo — delivering registration, payments, academic records, results, and CBT exams in a single system.

## Architecture

The monorepo is split into three applications:

- **School Website** — Next.js 16 public site with brochure content, admissions information, and downloadable PDFs (jsPDF / jspdf-autotable).
- **Student / Staff Portal** — Next.js dashboard for results, payments, profiles, and CBT exams.
- **Backend API** — Express 4.18 + Prisma 5.8 + PostgreSQL with JWT access/refresh token rotation, bcrypt, zod validation, helmet, sanitize-html, and express-rate-limit.

The data model is extensively documented in `architecture.md` with 21 models and 10 enums spanning a 8-role RBAC system.

## Key Features

- **Registration & Admissions** — multi-step admission forms with NIN, address, next-of-kin, and O-Level details.
- **Payments** — Paystack inline payment with dedicated callback and webhook endpoints for payment confirmation.
- **Academic Records** — results, resits, and graduation clearance workflows.
- **CBT Exams** — secure computer-based testing delivered through the portal.
- **Role-Based Access** — separate dashboards for SUPER_ADMIN, ADMIN, FINANCE, HOD, LECTURER, CLINICAL, STUDENT, and STAFF.

## Deployment Approach

Beyond code, the project includes a realistic deployment proposal targeting a Hetzner CPX42 VPS with PM2 and nginx reverse proxy, a `.com.ng` domain, and Google Workspace email — reflecting real commercial delivery rather than just a prototype.
