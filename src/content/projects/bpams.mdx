## Introduction

Religious and membership organizations manage people, communications, resources, and finances — often with the same generic tools a business would use, or worse, spreadsheets. BPAMS is a **high-performance church management system for UABCSP** that centralizes the organization's real operational needs: who is where, what's being communicated, where the money is, and what resources the community can access.

## The Problem

- **People administration is scattered.** Tracking priests, their roles, and their **postings** (assignments and rotations) across documents and email is hard to keep current and even harder to search.
- **Communications are fragmented.** An organization runs on timely messaging, but without a dedicated channel, important updates get lost in personal inboxes.
- **Finance lacks a dashboard view.** Monitoring organizational finances requires a clear, queryable picture — not a pile of statements.
- **Resources are locked away.** An e-library's value depends on being accessible; a library nobody can find is just archived files.
- **Clunky software kills adoption.** If the interface feels dated, staff won't use it no matter how complete it is.

## The Solution

BPAMS unifies these needs into **one platform with several clear, modern surfaces**:

- **Priest administration & postings** — profiles, assignments, and rotation tracking in one place, always current and searchable.
- **Finance tracking** — monitored through **dashboards** so leadership sees the money at a glance, not buried in exports.
- **Real-time messaging** — instant, organization-wide communication powered by **Supabase real-time channels**, so updates land immediately.
- **E-library** — a centralized digital resource the community can actually access.
- **A premium glassmorphism UI** built to feel modern and inviting, so the organization actually wants to use it.

## Architecture

Built with **Next.js 15 + React 19** and backed by **Supabase** for authentication, data, and real-time messaging. The design layer uses **Tailwind CSS 4** with **MUI 7** to deliver the glassmorphism aesthetic. The managed backend means the team gets production-grade auth and realtime without running their own infrastructure.

## Impact

- Priest **assignments and postings** are always current and queryable.
- **Real-time messaging** keeps the whole organization aligned on one channel.
- Finance is visible through **dashboards**, giving leadership the truth at a glance.
- The **e-library** becomes a real digital asset.
- A modern, polished interface drives **real adoption** — the difference between a system and one that's actually used.

## Lessons

- **Design drives adoption.** Investing in a premium UI paid off because software no one opens solves nothing.
- **Realtime is expected.** Members assume updates arrive instantly; Supabase channels made that the baseline, not a feature flag.
- **Managed infrastructure speeds delivery.** Using a BaaS for auth and realtime let the team focus on the domain features that make the product valuable.
