## Introduction

The BCS Charity Trust (Mercy Fellowship) needed a modern, trusted web presence — and, more importantly, a way to run its charitable operations with the transparency and accountability donors expect. A nonprofit's credibility depends on being able to show where money comes in and, just as convincingly, where it goes out. This platform pairs a polished public site with a complete admin portal to make that accountability real.

## The Problem

- **Donors need to trust the organization.** An official, polished site builds legitimacy, but it means little if donations can't be collected securely or if the organization can't demonstrate how funds are used.
- **Operations are spread thin.** Managing donations, beneficiaries, volunteers, campaigns, and disbursements across spreadsheets and emails is error-prone and opaque to leadership.
- **Accountability requires reporting.** A charity must be able to produce clear reports on what it collected and what it disbursed — even if it's a small team, the books must look credible.
- **Authorized access only.** Not every staff member should see every figure; roles and teams must keep financial data behind the right doors.

## The Solution

The platform is built around **one public face and one controlled admin core**:

- **Public site** — a polished Next.js front end with campaign information, stories, and a **live Paystack donation checkout**, so giving is secure, frictionless, and immediate.
- **Admin portal** — full management for **campaigns, beneficiaries, volunteers, CMS content, disbursements, and reports**.
- **Role/team-based access** — permissions keep financial and programmatic data scoped to the people who should see it.
- **Reporting & auditing** — the organization can generate reports and trace activity, the backbone of donor trust.

## Architecture

Built with **Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS**, with **Framer Motion** for refined transitions. Donations flow through a real **Paystack** payment integration, while the admin modules sit on a service layer so the entire workflow is demonstrable end-to-end before going live. It's structured to deploy directly to Vercel for production use.

## Impact

- Donors give through a **secure, branded channel**, raising confidence in the mission.
- Staff manage **programs and funds** in one system instead of spreadsheets.
- **Role-scoped access and reporting** give leadership — and the public — the visibility that sustains trust.
- The platform is **production-ready**, deployable with real payments live.

## Lessons

- **Trust is built with transparency.** The reporting and audit features are not bureaucracy — they are the product for a charity.
- **One brand, two surfaces.** Keeping public polish and admin control as separate apps on a shared foundation respected both audiences.
- **Payments are the trust moment.** Getting the Paystack flow secure and smooth was the single most important detail for converting visitors into donors.
